A little help for your camera roll

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Need a hand? Email support@studioec.nl with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please leave private photos out of your message.

How do I clean up?

Open a category. In similar groups, choose a keeper and add extras to the reject pile. For other items, use the reject control. Open your reject pile, review each item, then choose Delete and confirm with Apple Photos. You can undo your choices before deleting.

Does PhotoReject cost anything?

All scanning, reviewing and deleting tools are free. Optional PhotoReject Pro unlocks video compression with a one-time in-app purchase. There are no subscriptions or ads. Apple displays the local price before you confirm. Restore a prior purchase in Settings → PhotoReject Pro → Restore Purchases.

How does Pro compression work?

Choose a video, select up to 720p or 1080p, then make a smaller copy. Compare the preview and actual file sizes. Save the copy to Photos first. You can then choose Delete original, with confirmation; keeping it is the default. Favorites and protected originals are kept. Compression can reduce quality and change HDR appearance, frame rate or metadata. Some videos cannot be made smaller.

Why can I only see some photos?

You may have granted limited library access. Use Manage Access in the app or open iPhone Settings → Apps → PhotoReject → Photos to change it. Items outside that permission and hidden items cannot be scanned or reviewed.

What does the similar-photo scan find?

It compares locally available photos taken within approximately 15 minutes of each other and with a similar shape. Suggestions are visual matches, not a guarantee of duplicate files. Screenshots are reviewed separately. Photos stored only in iCloud may be skipped. Review every suggestion and choose your own keeper.

Can I recover a deleted photo?

Open Apple Photos → Recently Deleted, unlock it if asked, select the item and choose Recover. Apple normally keeps deleted items there for 30 days unless you permanently delete them sooner. PhotoReject does not empty Recently Deleted.

Why hasn't my storage changed?

Apple Photos manages storage and Recently Deleted. Space may not be reclaimed immediately, and iCloud optimization affects what is stored locally. PhotoReject does not claim an estimated number of gigabytes saved or clean other apps, contacts or system files.

Will deletion affect iCloud or my other devices?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting from your library can sync across devices. Read the final confirmation carefully.

Are favorites protected?

Yes. Favorites and items you mark Keep are excluded from the reject pile. Similar groups retain at least one keeper. If the library changes before deletion, the app rechecks your choices.

Where is the rating prompt?

The app asks Apple for a rating prompt after a successful cleanup, with a cooldown between requests. Apple decides when to display it. You can also review PhotoReject from its App Store page after release.

Can I use it offline?

Yes, locally available photos can be reviewed and scanned offline. Loading an original from iCloud, visiting support pages and App Store ratings require a connection.

PhotoReject requires iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Read the privacy policy for details about local storage and permissions.
