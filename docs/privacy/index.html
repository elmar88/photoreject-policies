Your photos.
Your business.

PhotoReject does not upload your photos or videos to Studio EC. Its cleanup and similarity analysis run on your device.

Who provides PhotoReject?

PhotoReject is provided by Studio EC (Elmar Van Cleynenbreugel). For privacy questions, email support@studioec.nl.

Photo-library permission

You can allow full access, select specific items, or decline access. PhotoReject reads accessible photos, videos and relevant metadata, including creation dates, dimensions, media type and favorite status, to display your library and suggest similar groups. Hidden items are excluded. Change access at any time in iPhone Settings.

What stays on your device

The app stores protected and rejected item identifiers, cleanup counts and the date of its last rating request in its local app storage. It caches mathematical image features locally to avoid repeating analysis. It does not identify people or create face profiles. Removing the app removes its local choices and cache; this does not restore photos already deleted from your library.

iCloud and deletion

Scanning uses locally available images and does not download originals from iCloud. If an original is unavailable in a detail view, you can choose to load it from iCloud through Apple Photos. This transfer is handled by Apple, not a PhotoReject server.

After you review and confirm a cleanup, PhotoReject asks Apple Photos to delete the selected items. With iCloud Photos enabled, that change can sync to other devices. Deleted items normally remain recoverable in Photos → Recently Deleted for 30 days, subject to Apple's behavior and your own actions.

No tracking or advertising

The app has no account system, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Studio EC does not collect app usage, location, contacts or photo-library contents through PhotoReject. Apple may separately process App Store and diagnostic information under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Optional Pro purchases and compression

Video compression runs on your device. Temporary output files are stored locally while you preview them and removed when you close the compression screen. A copy is added to your Photos library only when you choose Save. The original is retained unless you separately choose and confirm Delete original after the saved copy is verified.

Apple handles the optional one-time Pro purchase, payment and restoration. PhotoReject checks verified purchase entitlements on your device. Studio EC does not receive your payment details through the app. Apple processes purchase information under its own policies.

Ratings and support

After a successful cleanup, the app may ask Apple to display its standard rating prompt. Apple controls whether it appears. If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to that request. Please do not send private photos or other sensitive information unless needed for your question. You can ask us to delete support correspondence.

This website

These pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising or embedded third-party media. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following links or opening an email app is subject to those services' own policies.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the app's privacy practices change. Contact us with questions about your information or this policy.
