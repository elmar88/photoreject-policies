Free cleanup

Scanning, reviewing and deleting accessible photos and videos are free. PhotoReject has no advertisements, subscriptions or account requirement.

Optional PhotoReject Pro

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks video compression. Apple displays the price for your region before you confirm. Apple handles payment. Restore an existing purchase with the same Apple Account through Settings → PhotoReject Pro → Restore Purchases. For purchase or refund help, visit Apple’s purchase support. Apple handles refund requests under its applicable policies.

Your final decision

Similar-photo groups are suggestions. Review every item before deletion. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletions can sync to other devices. Photos normally keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days, unless they are permanently removed sooner. PhotoReject does not empty Recently Deleted.

Video compression

Compression is lossy: quality, HDR appearance, frame rate or metadata may change. Savings depend on the original; not every video can be made smaller. Preview the complete result before saving. PhotoReject keeps the original by default and offers deletion only after a new copy is saved and verified in Photos. Favorites and protected originals are retained. Apple Photos controls when storage space is reclaimed.
